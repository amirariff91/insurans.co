# Titles and descriptions, November 2026

**Branch:** `seo/titles-2026-11`, stacked on `perf/cwv-2026-11` (796a80b). It also includes the review fixes from `docs/seo-titles-review/hydrogen-review-raw.md`, the sitemap `<lastmod>` and the Corolla internal links. Not committed or deployed.

## Why

Evidence is in `docs/eeat-revamp-2026-11/round4/` of the E-E-A-T worktree (`model-coverage.md`, `search-intent.md`, `titles-hydrogen-raw.md`, `seo-readability.md` section 5).

- **Roadtax demand:** about 80% of model search demand contains "roadtax". The engine size often beats the bare name ("roadtax bezza 1.3" 200 a month against "roadtax bezza" 60), and model pages get almost no roadtax impressions today.
- **English insurance queries:** insurance queries are mostly English ("myvi insurance price" 100 a month against "insurans myvi" 20).
- **Low CTR at good positions:** model pages rank 5 to 8 with CTR under 1%. Every query has an AI Overview that gives the bare price, and the top results are all "Harga Road Tax dan Insurans <model>". The snippet therefore has to offer more: the yearly total with roadtax, and the engine size.
- **NCD table buried:** the NCD page gets "jadual/kadar ncd" impressions at positions 5 to 9 with 0% CTR, and its table was the 5th H2.
- **Generic comparison pages:** all comparison pages shared one template sentence, while searchers want a verdict.

## What changed

### Model pages (39)

These changes are in `src/pages/car-insurance/[model].astro`.

**Title.** The pattern is `Insurans & Roadtax <model> 2026: Anggaran RM<total>`.
- The total is insurance at NCD 55% plus Peninsular (Semenanjung) roadtax. It uses the same data and `rm()` rounding as the "Kos tahunan anggaran" box.
- All 39 titles carry "Anggaran" and fit 60 characters:
  - 29 use the full form.
  - 7 drop "2026".
  - 3 use a short name in the title only, from a new `titleName` data field: "Corolla Cross", "Mercedes C-Class" and "Honda Jazz/City HB". The H1 and the description keep the full name.
- The site suffix is dropped here through a new `siteSuffix` prop on `Base`.

**Description.** The pattern is `Insurance price & roadtax <model> <engine sizes> (anggaran, Semenanjung): insurans RM… + roadtax RM… = RM… setahun pada NCD 55%.`
- "≈" replaces "=" when roadtax has sen and the total is rounded (13 models).
- When the description would go over 155 characters, it ends `…/tahun, NCD 55%.` instead (6 models).
- 37 of 39 descriptions say "Semenanjung". C-Class and GLB do not fit it and say "(anggaran)" only.
- The closing "Semak ikut NCD 0–55%." no longer fits any model, so none carry it.

**Page and head tags.**
- og:title, twitter:title and the JSON-LD headline equal `<title>`.
- The H1 is "Harga insurans & roadtax <full name> 2026".
- A new lead sentence under the byline reads "Anggaran setahun (Semenanjung) untuk <model> <engine>: insurans … (NCD 55%) + roadtax … =/≈ …". It uses the existing Hero `description` slot.

**Data.** Honda City Hatchback's `titleOverride` is replaced by `searchName` "Honda City Hatchback (Jazz)", and its unused `descriptionOverride` is removed.

### Other pages

**NCD tool.**
- Title: "Jadual NCD Kereta 2026 (0–55%) & Kalkulator Insurans NCD", with no suffix by the same "only where it fits" rule.
- H1: "Jadual NCD & Kalkulator NCD Insurans Kereta 2026".
- The existing table moved to the top of the main column. It now has a visually hidden caption, `scope="col"` headers and `<th scope="row">` row labels at the same visual weight.
- Both calculators are unchanged.

**Hub.** The title is "Harga Insurans & Roadtax Kereta 2026: 39 Model", with the count taken from the data. The H1 and the JSON-LD headline now match it.

**Homepage.**
- Title: "Anggaran Harga Insurans Kereta Malaysia 2026".
- "anggaran premium 12 syarikat" is removed. The description now says what the site offers.

**Comparisons (15).**
- Each pair has its own `seoTitle` and `metaDescription` in `comparisons.json`. They are written only from that pair's `summary`, `chooseA` and `chooseB`.
- Every title, and so every H1, contains "insurans" or "takaful".
- Medical cover is called "kad perubatan", the term the page body uses.

**Guides.**
- `/guides/ncd-explained/`: the title, H1 and headline are all "NCD (No Claim Discount): Maksud, Pindah & Kesan Claim".
- `/literasi-kewangan/deductible-co-insurance/`: the H1 equals the title "Maksud Deductible & Co-Insurance (Contoh Bil)". The description opens with a one-sentence definition.
- `/guides/renew-car-insurance/`: the title is shortened to fit.

**Unchanged on purpose.**
- `/tools/kalkulator-insurans-rumah/`: within limits and not cut off.
- URLs, canonicals, robots, schema types, prices and other body copy.

### Sitemap `<lastmod>`

`astro.config.mjs` now passes a `serialize` function to `@astrojs/sitemap`. For each URL it uses the content date the page itself declares:
1. `article:modified_time` (163 URLs). This comes from data such as `premiumReviewed`, which gives the model pages 2026-11-11, or `asAt` for companies, comparisons, states and so on.
2. Otherwise the JSON-LD `dateModified` (1 URL).
3. Otherwise, for the roadtax, motorcycle-roadtax and home-insurance calculators, the `asAt` of the data file their byline shows (3 URLs).

That gives 167 of 179 URLs a `<lastmod>`. The other 12 (homepage, about, privacy, terms, hubungi, author page, hub indexes, NCD and coverage calculators) have no content date and get none. The build time is never used. The `<loc>` list is identical to the baseline (179 URLs).

### Internal links to /car-insurance/toyota-corolla/

- **Before:** 5 pages linked to it: the hub, Corolla Cross, Civic, Mazda 3 and Elantra.
- **Change:** `toyota-corolla` now appears in `relatedModels` for Vios and City (replacing Mazda 2 on both). The page shows 4 related models, so a fifth would not render. Saga keeps Axia: Saga owners compare with Axia, not Corolla. Mazda 2 keeps 4 inbound links.
- **After:** 7 pages link to it.
- **Homepage picker:** Corolla and Myvi are both already in the model `<select>`, and Myvi is also in its no-JS links.
- **Trade-off:** Mazda 2 loses two related-model links. Axia loses one, but is linked site-wide.
