# Research report: EPF/government insurance channels + public safety nets (all sources read 2026-09-12)

## FINDINGS

### 1. i-Lindung (KWSP/EPF) — verified
- **What**: online self-service channel (not a product) under KWSP's Pelan Perlindungan Ahli (PPA); launched 12 Jul 2022; lets members buy term-life/CI insurance & takaful from approved ITOs paying directly from EPF savings. Phase 2 (Feb 2024) added family coverage (spouse/children).
- **Which account**: **Akaun Sejahtera (formerly Akaun 2)** after the May-2024 Akaun 1/2/3 restructure. Annual deduction from Akaun Sejahtera balance; **Akaun 1 (Akaun Persaraan) untouched** — MoF reaffirmed this in a 13 Mar 2026 parliamentary reply (The Star). Over-55: eligible with ≥RM100 in Akaun 55/"Akaun Emas" (Prudential FAQ).
- **Product list (verified)**: Prudential — PRUGuard Family (term, death+TPD to RM200k/life, renewable to 75); PRUCare Family (43 CI, RM10k–RM200k); PruBSN Lindung Famili + PruBSN Cegah Famili (43 CI takaful). Etiqa — Term Plus / Term Takaful Plus (from RM12/yr, RM10k–RM200k, entry 17–65, children 14 days–16 yrs, 100% approval) + Critical Care Plus / Critical Care Takaful Plus (42 CI, from RM16/yr, +50% cancer benefit) (Etiqa newsroom 6 Feb 2024). FWD i-Lindung plans (term/CI) exist but the campaign page is JS-gated — names verified at title/meta level only. **Full 2026 operator list NOT confirmed** — kwsp.gov.my/i-lindung returned HTTP 403.
- **How to buy**: KWSP i-Akaun app → tab i-Lindung → choose plan → complete at ITO portal → authorize Akaun Sejahtera deduction (Prudential step guide). No medical exam; simplified questions. Policies are 1-year term, pure protection (no cash value); tax-relief eligible.
- **2026 status**: active; MoF studying extension of Akaun Sejahtera withdrawals to the **Base MHIT** (basic medical & health insurance/takaful) plan — announced in Budget 2026 (10 Oct 2025) + parliamentary reply Mar 2026. Not live as an i-Lindung product yet.
- **"i-Lindung PERKESO" — clarified**: **not a distinct product**. It conflates two separate things: KWSP i-Lindung (insurance/takaful purchase from Akaun Sejahtera) and PERKESO's self-employed scheme (Skim Perlindungan Sosial Pekerjaan Sendiri, "Lindung Kendiri"). No official source found linking the two; SPS contributions are paid through PERKESO channels (portal/Prihatin app), not via EPF accounts [INFERENCE from absence + structure of both schemes]. The 700/mo keyword is best served by a misconception/clarification section.

### 2. mySalam — verified, accuracy-critical
- **2026 status: RUNNING.** Official portal FAQ: "Kerajaan Malaysia sekali lagi telah bersetuju untuk meneruskan skim ini sehingga 2026" + claims window table showing 1 Jan–31 Dec 2026 eligibility / claims to 30 Jun 2027. Budget 2026 (Anwar, 10 Oct 2025): "inisiatif mySalam akan diteruskan pada 2026… lebih RM1.2 bilion tuntutan kepada 1.7 juta penerima". Deputy Finance Minister (Parliament, 27 Nov 2025): original RM2b Great Eastern fund diminishing, scheme extended "through the 2024 and 2026 Budget Speeches", long-term B40 model under study with BNM.
- **Administered by**: Great Eastern (GETB) under MoF initiative ("Diuruskan oleh" GETB + PIDM logos on portal footer). Still the same operator.
- **Eligibility 2026**: automatic via **STR (Sumbangan Tunai Rahmah)** recipient database from LHDN; married couples/OKU aged **18–65**, singles **21–65** (age at 1 Jan); notified by SMS; no registration. (Official FAQ faq_kelayakan — the previously flagged "50 diseases + age bands" are now confirmed on the official portal, resolving the repo's open flag.)
- **Benefits**: CI **RM8,000 one-off lifetime** on any of **50 critical illnesses in 12 groups** (official ci page); hospitalisation **RM50/day, max 14 days/yr = RM700/yr**, overnight stay required, government/university/military panel hospitals only (official hb page). Medical-device benefit "up to RM30,000 (e.g. stents)" appears only in The Vibes' Parliament report — **not verifiable on portal from here → flag**.
- **vs 2019 launch**: 36 → **50 diseases** (5 new categories — Rheumatoid, Chronic Skin, Blood, Mental, Rare Diseases — effective 1 Jan 2025, official FAQ16); eligibility moved from B40/Bantuan-based to STR-based. Payout amounts unchanged.
- **How to check/claim**: eligibility check + login at mysalam.com.my with MyKad (semakan/login intent stays portal-side); claims uploaded on portal with MyKad copy + CI form (12 form types by disease group, completed by treating specialist) or discharge note; processing ~10 working days; **2026-claims deadline 30 Jun 2027**; helpline 1-300-88-8938. Exclusions (self-harm, war, crime, alcohol/drugs, pregnancy) on official pages.

### 3. Perlindungan Tenang — verified
- BNM microinsurance/microtakaful initiative (2017): products "from a few ringgit a month", yearly renewable, lump-sum death benefit, claims paid within 5 working days with complete docs (bnm.gov.my/perlindungan-tenang).
- **Voucher history**: PTV 1.0 RM50 (2021) → 2.0 RM75 (2022) → **PTV 3.0 RM30 from 1 Sep 2025** — RM60m under Budget 2025, 2 million STR recipients first-come-first-served, products with ≥RM50/yr premium, check at myptv.my → redeem at mycoverage.my or walk-in; window "until fully redeemed or 31 Dec 2025, whichever earlier". **The RM75 figure is outdated (2022).** PTV 3.0 is a MoF/programme (gov), the Tenang framework is BNM's.
- **PTV 3.0 products** (mycoverage.my): AIA i-Starter; Allianz PerlindunganKu Allianz4All, A-Z Protect, Allianz Kasih Hayat; Chubb Protector Guard; GBR Qaseh Setia, Qaseh Bakti Plus; Great Eastern Takaful MikroSayang; HLA Stackable; HLM Takaful Tenang & Tenang 75; MSIG Mini PA; PruBSN Sakinah Plus; Tokio Marine TenangCare.
- Stamp-duty exemption for Perlindungan Tenang products **extended to 2028** (Budget 2026, astroawani). **2026 voucher round: NOT found → flag.**

### 4. PERKESO safety nets — verified (brief)
- **Skim Keilatan (Invalidity)**: 24-hour cover for any non-occupational cause; "keilatan" = permanent morbid condition leaving the worker unable to earn ≥1/3 of normal earnings, certified by Medical Board; requires age <60 at notice + minimum contributions; benefits = monthly invalidity pension/geran ilat (not a lump sum), Constant-Attendance Allowance (RM500/mo per parliament slide), survivors' pension, dialysis/rehab at panel. No disease list; no early-stage concept (perkeso.gov.my via search citations; direct page F5-blocked — see flags).
- **SPS (Skim Perlindungan Sosial Pekerjaan Sendiri)**, Akta 789/2017 "Lindung Kendiri": self-employed/gig (e-hailing, p-hailing, freelancers) covered for occupational injury/disease — not life/CI; contributions paid to PERKESO (baseline ~RM13.20/yr) with government matching schemes (SPS Padanan Caruman / SPS Madani). i-Saraan = the separate EPF voluntary-contribution track for the same audience.
- **Stacking story for the site**: PERKESO = statutory monthly income-replacement (occupation-based); mySalam = free B40 lump-sum CI + hospital allowance; i-Lindung = self-funded term/CI paid from own retirement savings; private = discretionary lump-sum at chosen sum assured. Complementary layers, each with distinct trigger, payout shape and funding.
